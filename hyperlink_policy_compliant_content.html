Content Review Policy (CRP)

The website is the face of the organization and therefore it is required to keep the content on the website current and up-to-date. This Content Review Policy defines the review process for the diverse content elements on the website.

The entire website content has been grammar checked, spelling checked, and reviewed for clarity and consistency.

Content is categorized and reviewed as follows:

  • Static Content: Reviewed quarterly to ensure accuracy and relevance.
  • News & Events: Reviewed immediately upon updates or expiry.
  • Tenders/E-procurement: Reviewed as per the tender lifecycle.

All content is validated for GIGW compliance before publication.
